    Fuel levy education

    How a fuel levy works

    Fuel levy weighting

    The share of your total operating cost that is fuel. Linehaul fleets sit higher than metro work with more waiting and handling. Use your own profit and loss. Cochrane's in South Australia publishes a fuel factor of 27 per cent.

    Base price

    The diesel price built into your current rates, set when you last agreed or reviewed them. Not today's price.

    Current price

    The benchmark now, usually an average over the review period. Team Global Express indexes its surcharge to the AIP national average and updates it weekly.

    Difference

    Current price minus base price, in cents per litre and as a percentage of the base.

    The calculation

    Levy equals the percentage change in price times the fuel weighting. Base 220 c/L, current 242 c/L, weighting 30 per cent: a 10 per cent rise times 30 per cent is a 3.0 per cent levy on the base rate.

    GST and fuel tax credits

    Most levies use prices excluding GST. Some also deduct the fuel tax credit. From 3 August 2026 that is 21.3 c/L for heavy vehicles on public roads: excise of 53.7 c/L less the road user charge of 32.4 c/L.

    The rules

    The Fair Work Commission's road transport contractual chain order on fuel cost recovery started operating again on 23 November 2026. It requires rates to be adjusted at least once a month for fuel cost increases measured against prices on or before 6 March 2026. An agreed fuel levy can meet the order if it is adjusted at least monthly, the other party has a copy of the order, and reasonable steps are taken to make sure it is enough. Use the 6 Mar 2026 base and a monthly review above to check yours. This is a guide, not legal advice: read the order and check your contracts.
