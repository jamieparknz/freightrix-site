THE BIG FIVE

Hume Freeway crash closes city bound lanes at Craigieburn

All city bound lanes on the Hume Freeway are closed between Donnybrook Road and Amaroo Road at Craigieburn following a serious collision. The closure is affecting both the Sydney to Melbourne corridor and the Melbourne to Brisbane via Newell corridor, and the incident was still active at time of publication. Drivers should exit at Wandong, Wallan or Donnybrook Road onto Epping Road eastbound and allow significant extra time.

AMSA imposes new conditions on Spirit of Tasmania after autopilot failure

AMSA has imposed new operating conditions on Spirit of Tasmania IV and Spirit of Tasmania V following another failure of the Kongsberg autopilot and steering system on Saturday 21 November. Bass Strait shippers and forwarders should contact Spirit of Tasmania directly to confirm whether any service changes apply to their bookings. Tonight's Geelong to Devonport sailing was already running approximately 26 minutes late at estimated departure.

Fuel cost recovery levy restarts today

The fuel cost recovery levy is back in effect from today, 23 November. Freight customers on eligible trucking jobs must again cover higher fuel costs, with payments flowing through the contracting chain to operators and drivers. Operators should confirm levy clauses are active in current contracts before dispatching jobs.

Great Western Highway long term closure continues into Blue Mountains

The emergency roadwork closure on Great Western Highway between Coxs River Road and Victoria Street at Mount Victoria has been in place since 5 March 2026 and remains active. This affects all freight moving between Sydney and the NSW central west, as well as the Barrier Highway route toward Adelaide. Operators on these corridors should confirm current diversion arrangements before dispatch.

Flooding and fire conditions on remote and coastal corridors

Donohue Highway is closed to all traffic between Glenormiston and the QLD and NT border due to flooding, and Sandover Highway in the NT is closed between the Ampilatwatja turn off and Austral Downs due to flooding and road damage. A grass fire at Tabbimoble is affecting visibility on the M1 Pacific Motorway on the Sydney to Brisbane coast route, and the Calder Highway has changed conditions in both directions between Nandaly and Bimbourie due to fire. Check current conditions before dispatching on any of these routes.

DIESEL AND FUEL LEVIES

National diesel averaged 242.3 cents per litre including GST as at 20 November, down 4.8 cents on the previous week and down 18.8 cents over 30 days, which may offer some modest relief on fuel levy calculations. However, the national price is still 66.2 cents per litre higher than a year ago, a 37.6 percent increase, so the levy restart today reflects a genuinely elevated cost base for operators. Operators setting or reviewing fuel levy rates should use the most current published figures from their relevant fuel index rather than the weekly movements alone.
