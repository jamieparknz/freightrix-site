■ Closed · +4.5 hrs  Road  SYD to MEL

B-double rollover shuts the Hume southbound at Tarcutta

AffectedHume Highway near Tarcutta, NSW. Southbound only.
WhoSydney to Melbourne road freight, and anything running south out of the Riverina.
ImpactAbout 4.5 hours on top of normal transit.
StatusStill closed. Reported 2.40am.
OutlookExpected to reopen around 9am.
What to do: Use the Olympic and Newell via Wagga Wagga, approved for B-doubles. It adds about 85km. Hold Melbourne departures until 6.30am if you can.

Heat speed limits slow the Trans-Australian line

AffectedAdelaide to Perth interstate rail.
WhoIntermodal customers with boxes due into Perth on Friday.
ImpactAbout 2 hours late into Kewdale.
StatusLimits lifted 9pm Wednesday. Trains are catching up.
OutlookMore heat is forecast Saturday. Expect the same again.
What to do: Tell Perth customers now. Book Saturday's freight a day earlier if it is time critical.

WhoContainer carriers with morning truck slots.

The 1962 Bedford that started a 300 truck fleet is back on the road

It carted wool bales out of the Riverina for eleven years, then sat in a shed for forty. Three generations of the family spent two winters getting it going again. On Saturday the founder's granddaughter drove it through the depot gate, past every driver on shift.
